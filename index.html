Privacy policy

Updated October 29, 2026

Family Calendar is an Android application for two adults to share appointments. This policy explains how the app handles account information, shared calendars, imports and reminders.

Account and appointment information

The app uses your email address, display name and account identifier for authentication, your profile and family membership. Passwords are submitted to Supabase Authentication over HTTPS. Session and verification material are stored using Android-backed SecureStore on your phone.

Your shared appointments can include titles, notes, locations you type, dates, times, timezones, recurrence rules, colors, reminder offsets and selected reminder recipients. Location text is appointment content; the app does not request GPS access. Avoid entering sensitive information you do not want your family member to see.

Sharing and service providers

Supabase provides authentication and cloud storage for accounts, family membership, appointments, preferences, reminder settings and import identifiers. Both members of a family can view and edit its appointments. Selecting reminder recipients changes who receives reminders, not who can see an appointment.

The app uses HTTPS and database authorization rules to restrict access to the signed-in family. Phone access, account credentials and device security also affect privacy. No security measure guarantees complete protection.

Family Calendar does not sell appointment data, include advertising SDKs, or use appointments for advertising. Service providers may process operational or security information, such as access logs, under their own policies. Google handles Play installation and updates under its own terms.

Optional calendar and file imports

With your permission, the Android calendar reader accesses Calendar Provider events to preview appointments in the date range you choose. It only reads the source calendars. It does not insert, edit or delete their events and does not request calendar write permission.

You can also choose an ICS file through Android's document picker. The app does not request broad storage or contacts access. Selected imported appointments and their source identifiers are copied into the app's Supabase database and become visible to your family member. Source identifiers help prevent repeat imports. This is a one-time copy, not continuous synchronization with the original calendar.

Phone storage and reminders

An app-private SQLite database stores cached appointments, family context, preferences and pending offline changes, separated by account. This SQLite data is not independently encrypted by the app. Android application isolation and your device's security controls protect local storage. App backup is disabled.

Signing out cancels the app's scheduled reminders but retains cached data and pending changes on that phone so the same account can resume later. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes those local copies and unsynchronized changes; it does not delete your cloud account or synchronized appointments.

When you enable notifications, the app schedules local reminders for selected recipients. Android can display appointment content in notifications, including on the lock screen according to your notification settings. Android permissions, battery restrictions, force-stop and background limits can affect delivery.

Retention and account deletion

Cloud account and calendar information is retained to provide the service until removed through the app or an account/data-deletion request. Deleted-event markers, import identities and operation receipts support synchronization and safe retries; an account-deletion request includes review and removal of associated data.

To request account deletion, email the support address below from your registered email address with the subject Family Calendar account deletion request. Requests are handled manually after account ownership is verified. Opening the request button only opens your email composer; it does not send a message or delete an account automatically. Do not send passwords, verification codes or appointment details.

Deleting an account and its associated data can affect shared appointments. The operator will explain the scope before completing deletion. Copies already exported or independently retained by another person cannot be recalled. Clear app storage or uninstall on your devices to remove their local caches and pending changes. Provider backups and security logs may persist for their applicable retention periods; any retention that applies to your request will be explained.

Audience, changes and contact

Family Calendar is intended for adult personal users and is not directed to children. The privacy policy may change when the app's data handling changes; its updated date identifies this version.

For app support, privacy questions or account/data-deletion requests, contact the public support email below.
