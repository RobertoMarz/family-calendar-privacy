Request account and data deletion

You can request deletion of your Family Calendar account and associated data here even if you have uninstalled the app.

Email martygamingbuisness@gmail.com from your registered email address.

Subject: Family Calendar account deletion request

Open an account-deletion request email

What to include

  1. Your registered Family Calendar email address and a statement that you want your account and associated data deleted.
  2. If you only want specific appointment data removed, describe that request without including private appointment details in the first email.

Do not send your password, verification codes or session tokens. If the button cannot open an email app, send the request manually to the address above. No app installation or sign-in to this page is required.

What happens next

Requests are handled manually. The operator will verify account ownership and explain the scope before completing deletion. Sending an email does not immediately delete an account.

Account deletion includes review and removal of associated account/profile data, calendar data, import identifiers and synchronization records. Shared appointments may be affected. Copies already exported or independently retained by another person cannot be recalled.

Clear app storage or uninstall on your devices to remove local caches and unsynchronized changes. Uninstalling by itself does not delete cloud data. Provider backups and security logs may persist for their applicable retention periods; any retention that applies to your request will be explained.
